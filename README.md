# 🐾 PawStory – Pet Adoption Website

### Every Paw Deserves a Loving Home ❤️

PawStory is a pet adoption platform designed to connect rescued animals with loving families. It provides a simple and user-friendly experience for discovering pets, exploring their details, and submitting adoption applications.

## 🌟 Project Overview

PawStory aims to make pet adoption easier by bringing pets and potential adopters together through a simple digital platform.

Users can explore available pets, learn about their characteristics, save their favourite pets, and complete a structured adoption application.

## ✨ Features

- 🐶 **Browse Pets:** Explore rescued dogs, cats, rabbits and other pets.
- 🔍 **Search & Filter:** Search pets by breed, name or shelter.
- ❤️ **Favourites:** Save pets you are interested in adopting.
- 🐾 **Pet Details:** View pet information, age, gender, vaccination status and personality.
- 🏠 **Adoption Application:** Complete a three-step adoption form.
- 📋 **Applicant Information:** Provide personal details, home environment and lifestyle information.
- 🩺 **Care & Health Details:** Share veterinary arrangements, care plans and reasons for adoption.
- ✅ **Application Confirmation:** Receive a confirmation screen after submitting an application.

## 📱 Adoption Process

The adoption process is divided into three steps:

**Step 1 – About You**
- Full name
- Phone number
- Location
- House or apartment
- Previous pet ownership

**Step 2 – Home & Lifestyle**
- Household members
- Living environment
- Daily routine
- Sleeping and containment arrangements

**Step 3 – Care & Final Review**
- Veterinary arrangements
- Healthcare budget
- Training plans
- Reason for adoption
- Final confirmation
